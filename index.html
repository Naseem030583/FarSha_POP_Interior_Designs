What is POP (Plaster of Paris) False Ceiling?

Plaster of Paris, commonly known as POP, is a versatile building material made from gypsum. When mixed with water, it forms a smooth paste that can be molded into various shapes and designs. False ceilings, also called drop ceilings or suspended ceilings, are secondary ceilings installed below the main ceiling to enhance aesthetics and functionality.

POP false ceilings have become an essential element of modern interior design, offering both beauty and practical benefits. They provide the perfect canvas for creative lighting solutions, help conceal wiring and pipes, and add a touch of elegance to any room.

Why Choose POP False Ceiling?

Highly Customizable

POP can be molded into any shape, pattern, or design. From simple geometric patterns to intricate curves and decorative elements, the possibilities are endless.

Perfect for Lighting

Ideal for installing recessed lights, LED strips, cove lighting, and chandeliers. Creates beautiful ambient lighting effects that enhance your space.

Fire Resistant

POP is naturally fire-resistant, providing an extra layer of safety. It doesn't support combustion and helps slow down fire spread.

Sound Insulation

Excellent acoustic properties help reduce noise from upper floors and external sources, creating a quieter, more peaceful environment.

Temperature Control

Acts as an insulator, keeping rooms cooler in summer and warmer in winter. Helps reduce energy costs by maintaining consistent room temperature.

Cost-Effective

More affordable than many alternatives like gypsum or wood. Offers great value with prices ranging from ₹50-150 per square foot.

Long-Lasting

With proper maintenance, POP ceilings can last 15-20 years or more. Durable and resistant to wear and tear.

Conceals Imperfections

Hides unsightly wires, pipes, air conditioning ducts, and structural imperfections. Creates a clean, finished look.
