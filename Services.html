POP False Ceiling Design

Transform your ceilings into works of art with our custom POP false ceiling designs. From simple and elegant to elaborate and ornate, we create ceilings that enhance your space's aesthetics and functionality.

Types of False Ceiling We Offer:

Simple & Elegant Designs

Clean lines and minimalist patterns perfect for modern homes. Ideal for those who prefer understated elegance.

  • Single-layer designs
  • Border ceilings
  • Flat panel designs

Plus-Minus / Step Designs

Creates depth with raised and recessed sections. Popular choice for living rooms and master bedrooms.

  • Two-level designs
  • Multi-step patterns
  • 3D depth effects

Cove Ceiling with LED

Hidden LED strips in recessed grooves create stunning ambient lighting effects.

  • Indirect lighting
  • Color-changing LEDs
  • Mood lighting options

Geometric Patterns

Bold shapes and contemporary designs that make a statement.

  • Circular designs
  • Square patterns
  • Hexagonal sections
